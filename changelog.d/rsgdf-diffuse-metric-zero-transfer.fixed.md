- The Cholesky-first SR/LR GDF fit (#179) now evaluates the zero-transfer metric rows of diffuse auxiliary functions (every exponent at most omega^2) as a plain reciprocal-space Coulomb sum, as in Ye & Berkelbach's RSGDF. The previous range-separated assembly lost most digits of these entries to the finite short-range G=0 subtraction, and the full-rank fit amplified that architecture-dependent rounding: LiH/STO-3G (2,2,2) differed by 1.2e-7 Ha between Linux and macOS (vibe-qc 0.18.4). Linux and macOS now agree to about 4e-11 Ha on LiH and MgO at Gamma, and both match PySCF to 1e-7 Ha.
