# χ-CCM / aiccm2026dev-b decisions and open questions

Last updated: 2026-09-05.

## Decisions

D131 (issue #704, partial): repair the dominant multi-k nuclear-attraction
symmetry defect at its shared one-electron source. The alpha-only nuclear
image radius omitted displaced, smeared Gaussian-product interactions.
`ewald_erfc_lattice_options` now requires the actual basis/system and extends
only the nuclear image set using the output radius, relative centre offsets
and the Gaussian-product erfc range. Energy and analytic V_ne derivative
callers share this support rule. The prototype erfc-factor bound is not a
total-integral error bound or physical-source certificate. The three-character
He2 control now passes the unchanged native half-translation sewing gate;
the two-character control retains a smaller residual and still fails closed.
Single frozen bands in a degenerate pair are admitted only when their actual
mask projection is invariant. No representative CCSD(T) or total-gradient
capability is enabled by this partial repair.

D130 (issue #703): reuse the shared native full-k RHF state and one-Seitz
frozen/active/virtual sewing validator through a bounded chi-owned numerical
snapshot bridge. Preserve the recorded F/S/C/epsilon/occupations, Cartesian
k coordinates and weights; never construct a passing Fock matrix from the
eigenvalues or silently regenerate a missing full mesh. The initial envelope
is all-electron, zero-smearing, 3D four-center RHF. Exact frozen occupied band
indices and a positive insulating-gap floor are caller declarations. Native
stationarity/metric/Aufbau gates are unchanged, so an ordinary converged SCF
with looser residual tolerances can be refused. A numerical snapshot is not a
native SCF capture or integral-source certificate. Its provenance digest binds
the caller identity, chi convention and masks without authenticating that
declaration. See the [snapshot contract](experimental/aiccm2026dev_b_symmetry.md#restricted-native-snapshot-bridge).

D129 (issue #697): represent the spatial quotient and its integer lattice
cocycle separately, using `U_g U_h = T_ell(g,h) U_products[g,h]`. Validate
every supplied quotient product and triple, not just a few generators.
The identity representative must have zero translation. The occupied witness
checks the same law plus finite translation order, commutation and conjugation
in the actual localized C/S gauge, preserving general matrix mixing. This
certifies a representation of the supplied closed spatial set, not completeness
of that set, an antiunitary group or the physical Fock/frozen/virtual spaces.
PAO/PNO/TNO/amplitude covariance and representative energy execution stay gated.
The [group specification](experimental/aiccm2026dev_b_symmetry.md#spatial-group-and-occupied-cocycle)
states the resource limits and the native multi-k phase comparison.

D128 (issue #683): admit a single occupied-space symmetry action only after
checking the actual localized coefficients and overlap. The projection
`D_g = C^dagger S U_g C` retains all complex entries. A unique column-wise
phase-permutation fit is exposed only when its Frobenius residual passes the
declared tolerance; general mixing has no scalar permutation. The result
binds numerical C/S and the D127 action, with explicit-array admission before
large copies. Positive-definite overlap is the initial supported envelope.
This is a one-operation diagnostic: D129 adds the spatial-group check; orbital
reference-cell offsets, domain/amplitude covariance and representative energy
execution remain open. See the [symmetry specification](experimental/aiccm2026dev_b_symmetry.md#occupied-space-action).

| D39 | Make the RSGDF reciprocal mesh respect the declared periodic dimensionality. | Low-dimensional χ-CCM routes embed chains and slabs in large vacuum-padding axes. | D40 | Stream the canonical χ-CCM RI-MP2 energy contraction in native OpenMP code. | The earlier Python loop materialised one \(O(n_o^2 n_v^2)\) `oovv` scratch tensor per virtual k block for every occupied k pair. ## Current measured evidence

The committed benchmark was run on the alternating H4/STO-3G chain with a
40-bohr transverse simulation lattice. For cyclic lengths 1, 2, and 4, the
new energy per cell was respectively -2.0733777009, -2.1648043358, and
-2.1719609271 Ha. A direct call to the full Gamma-centred character mesh was
identical to all printed digits at every length. The historical `union12`
CCM gave -2.1615967328, -2.1618792916, and -2.1707034746 Ha/cell. The
separately developed symmetric `aiccm2026dev-a` weighting gave the same displayed
sequence in this 1D case. This is useful comparison evidence, not a
correctness proof. New-route wall times in the archived current-main run were
22.58, 20.61, and 97.23 seconds; the four-cell jump reflects all-k-pair
exact-exchange fitting.

That H4 ladder used the `ri` backend. The backend-crossing smoke benchmark
uses H2/STO-3G in an 8 x 12 x 12 bohr cell at a (2,1,1) cyclic mesh. RHF
energies are -1.1213506456 (`four_center`), -1.1213722182 (`ri`), and
-1.1213943367 Ha/cell (`rijcosx`). PBE0 energies are -1.1554208287,
-1.1554589526, and -1.1554644822 Ha/cell in the same order. These are actual
runs from 2026-06-21; the maximum spread is 43.7 microhartree for RHF and
