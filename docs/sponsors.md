## Acknowledgements

Beyond direct financial support, vibe-qc is built on a stack of
open-source libraries whose maintainers deserve credit:
[libint](https://github.com/evaleev/libint),
[libxc](https://libxc.gitlab.io/),
[Eigen](https://eigen.tuxfamily.org/),
[spglib](https://github.com/spglib/spglib),
[FFTW3](https://www.fftw.org/),
[libecpint](https://github.com/robashaw/libecpint),
[pybind11](https://github.com/pybind/pybind11),
[NumPy](https://numpy.org/), and
[ASE](https://wiki.fysik.dtu.dk/ase/). The full third-party
license list is bundled in
[license inventory](https://github.com/vibe-qc/vibe-qc/blob/main/docs/license.md).
